# Brief N: starting a chapter again

Read `common.md` in this directory first. Write your draft to
`docs/notes/chapter-01/drafts/revision/N.md`.

These words sit at the foot of every chapter, after the reflection and above the links to the
next chapter. They belong to the course's shell, not to Chapter 1, so use no fact from the fact
sheet beyond what is listed here. Plain text throughout: no Markdown, no backticks.

Facts:

- Every prediction the learner commits in a chapter stays as the record of what they expected:
  there is no way to re-answer it once the lab has answered.
- The chapter keeps the learner's predictions, sorts and challenge work in their browser.
- Starting the chapter again clears all of it, for this chapter only, and cannot be undone.
- It takes two presses: the first asks the learner to confirm.

## startAgain

The button that begins it, a few words, no full stop: start this chapter again.

## startAgainNote

One or two sentences above the button, ending with a full stop: the chapter keeps your
predictions, sorts and challenge work in your browser; starting again clears them all.

## startAgainConfirm

The button that confirms, a few words, no full stop: clear everything in this chapter.

## startAgainCancel

The button that backs out, a few words, no full stop: keep my work.

## startAgainDone

A sentence shown after clearing, ending with a full stop: this chapter's work is cleared.
