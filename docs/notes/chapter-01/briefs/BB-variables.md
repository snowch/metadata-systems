# Brief BB: the three variables

Chapter 1 says, just before its first code cell: "In the cells, `storage`, `warehouse` and
`reporting` name the shop's three systems: object storage, the warehouse and the reporting tool."
The author asked whether these should be called variables. They should: "name" leaves the reader
to work out what the words are.

Write the sentence following `docs/style.md` and `AGENTS.md`. British English. No em dashes. Do
not mention a lab, an engine, a simulator, Jupyter, JupyterLite or Pyodide. The reader is "you", a
data engineer who reads Python.

## Facts

- In the code cells, three Python variables stand for the shop's three systems.
- `storage` is object storage, `warehouse` is the warehouse, and `reporting` is the reporting tool.
- Keep the three names in backticks, exactly as written.

## Slot

- **variables**: one sentence, with all three facts.
