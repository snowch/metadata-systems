1. Say what happens on the platform each night, and what you cannot see of it: the jobs, when they are due to run, and any notes.

2. The platform has three systems: object storage, which holds files; a warehouse, which holds tables; and a reporting tool, which holds a dashboard. Every night, an export from the shop's checkout and catalogue writes three files to object storage. Then jobs read the files, write the tables and refresh the dashboard. You can read everything the three systems hold. You cannot see the export, the jobs, when they are due to run, or any note anybody kept.

3. Before you have sat down, the head of the shop sends you a message: "Thursday's revenue looks wrong."

4. Each night, in the early hours of the next day, the export writes the three files to object storage again, then jobs write the tables and refresh the dashboard.

5. The head of the shop has sent you a message: "Thursday's revenue looks wrong."

Applied with one addition to string 1, for a missing fact: "the export," in the objectives' list of what you cannot see.
