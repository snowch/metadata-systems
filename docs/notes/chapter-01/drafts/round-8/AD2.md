## qLab

Every figure on the page runs the Metadata Lab, a small data platform written for this course. It comes with the page, as code, and runs in your browser: there is nothing to install, open or sign in to. The lab holds the shop with data from Monday 7 to Sunday 13 September 2026: files, tables, dashboard and programs in SQL. The shop and week are invented for the course. Each night the programs run; the last night ends early on Monday 14 September, before you start work.

The lab runs the programs' SQL with its own query engine. Nothing about the shop is stored: each page load builds the shop's data and runs the whole week in memory. All readers see the same rows and times from that week, never today or your clock. You use the lab only through figures on the page: each asks the lab something and gets its answer. Your browser keeps your predictions, choices and answers to challenges. Nothing you do leaves your browser.
