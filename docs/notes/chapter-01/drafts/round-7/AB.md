## labNote

The Metadata Lab is a data platform written for this course. It comes with the page and runs in your browser: nothing to install or sign in to. The shop holds files, tables, a dashboard with real rows and programs written in SQL. The lab holds one week of the shop: its first week online, Monday 7 to Sunday 13 September 2026. The programs run each night. The last night ends early Monday 14 September.

The lab reads and runs the programs' SQL with a query engine. Nothing about the shop is stored: each time the page loads, the lab builds the shop's data and runs the whole week, night by night, in memory. Rows and times are the same for every reader. Times shown by a figure come from the week, not today or your clock.

You use it through figures, each asking the lab something and showing its answer. A figure showing storage reads what the lab's storage records. The lab runs a query you build with the same query engine. The lab runs the week again when you change the shop. Your browser keeps your predictions, choices and answers. Nothing you do leaves your browser.
