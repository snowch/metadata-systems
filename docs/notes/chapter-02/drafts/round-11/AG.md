## labUse

The map, the shop's week and the dashboard only show you the shop: you read them. You ask the lab something in the other figures: you choose an answer, an asset or the parts of a query, and press a button. The lab works out what the figure shows from the shop's data. Many figures ask you to predict or choose before the lab answers. You cannot see the shop's programs, only what they leave behind, so you work out what happened from the figures.
