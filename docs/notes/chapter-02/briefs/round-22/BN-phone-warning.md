## BN2: one or two sentences, added at the end of the folded "Time and memory" details

Facts, measured on 13 October 2026 with a browser held to a third of one processor core:
1. With a fast mobile network (9 Mbps), the first run of every cell took 51 seconds; with a slow
   one (1.6 Mbps), 98 seconds.
2. After a reload, the files come from the browser's store, and running every cell again took 36
   to 40 seconds.
Say that the phone was emulated, not a real one: "on a computer set to run like a slow phone" or
similar.
